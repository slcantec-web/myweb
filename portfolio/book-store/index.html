Chapter 01The Shop

Chosen by people who read them

Inkwell & Folio opened in 2012 on the idea that a bookstore should be curated, not comprehensive — every title on the shelf was actually read and argued for by someone on staff, new release or twenty years old.

We buy used books in person, at the counter, and check every one for condition before it goes back out. Nothing ex-library, nothing water-damaged, nothing you wouldn't want to give as a gift.

  • New releases and staff-picked backlist, side by side
  • Hand-vetted used books bought in-store
  • Handwritten shelf-talkers on every staff pick
  • Special orders for anything not in stock
