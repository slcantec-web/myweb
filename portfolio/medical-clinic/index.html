Tab A — The Clinic

Built around the chart, not the visit

Fernwood opened in 2013 with a simple rule: every patient keeps the same provider and the same paper-and-digital chart for as long as they're with us, so nothing gets lost between visits and nothing has to be explained twice.

We cap each provider's panel deliberately, well below the regional average, so there's still room on the schedule when you actually need to be seen — not just when there's an opening three weeks out.

  • One assigned provider for every patient
  • Same-day sick visits held daily
  • In-house lab draws, no separate appointment
  • Capped patient panels per provider
Tab B — Care

What we treat

From an annual physical to a same-day fever, every visit starts with your actual chart, not a blank form.

A

Primary Care Visits

Annual physicals, checkups, and ongoing care with the same provider each time.

B

Same-Day Sick Visits

Slots held open every weekday morning for fevers, infections, and things that can't wait.

C

Preventive Screenings

Bloodwork, vitals, and age-appropriate screenings tracked against your history, not just this visit.

D

Chronic Disease Management

Diabetes, hypertension, and other ongoing conditions managed with regular check-ins, not just refills.

E

Pediatric Care

Well-child visits, immunizations, and sick visits for kids, seen by a provider who knows the family.

F

Telehealth Visits

Video visits for follow-ups and simple concerns, pulled from the same chart as your in-person care.
