🦷 Tooth No. 01 — The Studio

Every visit starts with the chart

Meridian opened in 2015 around one decision: go fully digital. Intraoral scanners replaced impression trays, and an in-house mill means a crown that used to take two appointments and a temporary now happens in one visit.

Your chart tracks every tooth individually — what's been treated, what's being watched, and what's due for a recall — so your hygienist and dentist are always looking at the same picture.

  • Digital intraoral scanning, no impression trays
  • In-house same-day crown milling
  • Low-dose digital panoramic imaging
  • Nitrous and oral sedation available

🦷 Tooth No. 07 — Good to Know

Questions

We're in-network with most major dental plans. Bring your card to your first visit and we'll verify your benefits before treatment.
We scan the tooth digitally, design the crown chairside, and mill it in our on-site lab while you wait — most patients are done within two to three hours, in one visit.
Nitrous oxide is available for any visit, and oral sedation can be arranged in advance for longer or more involved procedures.
We recommend a first visit by age one, or within six months of the first tooth coming in — earlier visits are short and mostly about getting comfortable.
