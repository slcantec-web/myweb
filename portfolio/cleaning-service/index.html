✓Task 01 — The Team

Every room follows the same checklist

Bristle & Bright opened in 2014 on a simple idea: cleaning shouldn't depend on which technician shows up. Every room type has a written checklist, signed off by the technician and left on the counter, so you can see exactly what got done.

All staff are background-checked, insured, and trained on the same eco-friendly product line — nothing harsh, nothing that needs the windows open for an hour after we leave.

  • Written, room-by-room cleaning checklist
  • Background-checked & insured technicians
  • Eco-friendly supplies, included on every visit
  • Photo log included for commercial accounts
✓Task 02 — Services

What we clean

From a weekly standing appointment to a one-time deep clean, every job runs off the same checklist system.

Standard Cleaning

Kitchens, bathrooms, floors, and dusting, checked off room by room, on a schedule you set.

Deep Cleaning

Baseboards, inside appliances, and grout — the once-a-season reset most cleanings skip.

Move-In / Move-Out

A full top-to-bottom clean timed around your lease or closing date, empty-home pricing.

Office & Commercial

After-hours or early-morning cleaning for offices and storefronts, with a signed checklist left on-site.

Recurring Maintenance

Weekly, biweekly, or monthly visits at a locked-in rate — pause or reschedule any time.

Post-Construction Cleanup

Dust, debris, and finish work removed so a freshly built space is actually move-in ready.
