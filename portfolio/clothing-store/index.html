Look 01 — The Studio

Fewer pieces, chosen carefully

Fold & Form opened in 2018 in a converted print studio, and the racks are still deliberately sparse — every piece is chosen by our two buyers, not a merchandising algorithm.

We work with small independent makers wherever we can, and every capsule is sized to sell out, not sit marked down at the end of the season.

  • Small seasonal capsules, not constant drops
  • 30+ independent makers stocked
  • In-store tailoring & alterations
  • Personal styling appointments
