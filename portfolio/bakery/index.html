The Bakehouse

Hearth & Crumb opened in 2015 with a single deck oven and a sourdough starter we still feed every morning — it's older than the bakery itself, brought over from the founder's grandmother's kitchen.

Everything is mixed, shaped, and baked on-site starting at 4am, so the sourdough that hits the case at 7 finished proofing three hours earlier, not three days earlier in a warehouse.

  • Naturally leavened, long-fermented sourdough
  • Butter laminated fresh each morning
  • Custom cakes built to order
  • Wholesale accounts for local cafés
