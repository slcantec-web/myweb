Course 01

The Kitchen

Ember & Copper opened in 2012 around a single idea: build the kitchen in the middle of the dining room and cook everything over the same wood fire, where guests can watch it happen.

The tasting menu changes with what the fire and the season allow, and every table gets the same seven courses, paced by the kitchen, not the clock.

  • Open, wood-fired show kitchen
  • Seasonal seven-course tasting menu
  • Extensive natural wine list
  • Private dining room for up to 12
