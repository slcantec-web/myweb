Bunch 01 — The Studio

Sourced daily, not warehoused weekly

Petal & Twine opened in 2015 with a standing 5am order at the regional flower market — we buy what's actually good that morning, not a pre-set list, so the bucket behind the counter changes with the season instead of a catalog.

Every stem is cut, conditioned, and hand-tied in-house. We skip floral foam entirely and build arrangements the way a florist would have forty years ago — with twine, a good knife, and an eye for what's in season.

  • Sourced from regional flower farms each morning
  • Hand-tied, foam-free arrangements
  • Same-day local delivery on most orders
  • Walk-in cooler stocked fresh, not held over
