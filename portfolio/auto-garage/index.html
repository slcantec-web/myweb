Bay 01 — The Garage

Every car gets photographed before it gets touched

Torque & Bay opened in 2007 on one rule: nothing gets repaired until you've seen why. Every inspection comes back with timestamped photos of the actual part in question, sent to your phone before we ever call with a quote.

Six bays, six ASE-certified techs, and a shop policy that no add-on gets billed without your explicit approval first — not a checkbox buried in the paperwork.

  • ASE-certified technicians on every bay
  • Digital inspection with photos before any work
  • Loaner cars available for longer repairs
  • OEM-spec parts, no cut corners
