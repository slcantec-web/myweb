Joint 01 — The Workshop

One floor, one wood, no particleboard

Grain & Joint opened in 2013 in a single converted mill floor, and everything still happens under that one roof — milling, joinery, assembly, and finishing, by the same small crew from first cut to delivery.

Every joint is cut to be seen, not hidden under a panel. If you turn a piece over, the joinery is the same on the underside as it is on the front — that's the whole standard we hold ourselves to.

  • Solid hardwood only — no MDF, no particleboard
  • Hand-cut dovetail & mortise-and-tenon joinery
  • Locally sourced walnut, white oak & maple
  • Every commission signed and dated underneath
