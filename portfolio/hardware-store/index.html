Bin 01 — The Store

Organized by project, not by brand

Bolt & Beam opened in 2003 with a simple rule: aisles get organized by what you're trying to fix, not by which manufacturer paid for the endcap. If you're rehanging a door, everything you need is in one aisle, not four.

Most of the counter staff worked the trades before they worked the counter — plumbers, electricians, a cabinet maker — so the advice you get is usually the same advice they'd give a coworker.

  • Loose fastener bins, priced by the piece
  • Staff who've worked the trades they're advising on
  • In-store key cutting & paint matching
  • Daily & weekend tool rental
