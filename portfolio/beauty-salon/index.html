Level 01 — The Studio

Where formulas are kept, not guessed

Every client at Tone & Texture gets a formula card — the exact levels, tones, and processing times used on your hair, kept on file so your colorist (and any colorist covering for them) can repeat it precisely next time.

We're a five-chair studio inside a converted textile loft, chosen for the north-facing windows: the same light your color has to look right in when you leave.

  • Formula cards kept for every client
  • Natural daylight color-matching stations
  • Cruelty-free, ammonia-light color lines
  • Private consultation room
