0x01 — The Shop

Every machine gets logged before it gets touched

Kernel & Core opened in 2015 on a simple idea: a repair shop should run like a lab. Every drop-off gets a ticket, a benchmark, and a technician who explains what the numbers actually mean, not just a price.

Custom builds go together on an open bench, cable-managed properly, and run a full 24-hour burn-in under load before anything ships out the door.

  • Certified repair technicians on every shift
  • In-house PC building & 24-hour burn-in testing
  • Genuine & compatible parts in stock
  • Free diagnostics on every drop-off
