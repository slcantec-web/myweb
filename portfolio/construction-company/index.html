Phase 01 — The Company

Every phase inspected before the next begins

Frame & Ledger opened in 2005 on a rule that's still enforced on every job: no framing crew moves to drywall, and no drywall crew moves to finish, until the previous phase is inspected and signed off — by us, and by you if you want to walk it.

Our framing, electrical rough-in, and finish carpentry stay in-house rather than subcontracted out entirely, so the same standards apply from foundation to final walkthrough.

  • Licensed, bonded & insured general contractor
  • Documented phase inspections, photo-logged
  • Dedicated project manager on every job
  • In-house framing & finish carpentry crew
