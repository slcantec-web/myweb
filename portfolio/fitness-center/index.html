Round One

The Gym

Ten Count opened in 2013 in a converted freight warehouse, and we kept the concrete floor on purpose — it's the same floor our first ten members trained on. We're not a big-box chain. Classes cap at 16 people so coaches can actually watch your hands.

Every session runs on the same clock a fight does: three-minute work, one-minute rest, repeated until the bell says otherwise.

  • Certified boxing & strength coaches
  • 16-person class cap
  • Open gym access included
  • Kids & teens program
Round Two

Programs

Pick your lane, or rotate through all of them — membership covers every program on the floor.

01

Boxing Fundamentals

Stance, footwork, and combinations on the bag before anyone puts on sparring gloves.

02

Strength & Conditioning

Barbell strength work built around the same rounds structure — three minutes on, one minute off.

03

Sparring Lab

Controlled, coached sparring for members cleared by a coach. Headgear and mouthguard required.

04

Open Gym

Bags, racks, and jump ropes, no class structure — just you, a timer, and the floor.

05

Youth Program

Ages 8–15. Footwork, discipline, and non-contact bag work three afternoons a week.

06

Private Coaching

One-on-one mitt work and technique correction, booked directly with your coach.
