Listing No. 01 — The Brokerage

One agent, start to close

Parcel & Post opened in 2010 on a simple complaint we kept hearing: sellers meet a senior agent at the listing appointment, then get handed off to whoever's free for the showings. Here, the agent who prices your home is the agent who negotiates the offer.

We keep our roster deliberately small — nine agents, no teams stacked three deep — so every file gets one person who actually knows the house, the block, and the buyer on the other end.

  • Free, no-obligation home valuations
  • One agent from listing to closing table
  • Professional photography included on every listing
  • No dual agency, ever
