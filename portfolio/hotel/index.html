Key No. 01 — The House

Twelve rooms, kept deliberately small

Linden House opened in 2016 inside a 1938 townhouse we spent a year restoring rather than gutting — the oak staircase, the leaded windows, and the fireplace in the parlour are all original, not reproductions.

We stopped adding rooms at twelve on purpose. Past that, a front desk stops recognizing faces, and recognizing faces is most of what we're selling.

  • 12 individually furnished rooms, no two alike
  • 24-hour staffed front desk, always a person
  • Locally sourced breakfast included nightly
  • Original 1938 architectural details throughout
