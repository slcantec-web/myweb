Rx No. 01 — The Apothecary

Filled correctly, not just quickly

Millbrook opened in 2009 inside a restored 1948 corner drugstore, and we kept the original marble dispensing counter on purpose — it's a reminder that a pharmacist used to know every name that walked through that door, and ours still do.

Every prescription is checked by a pharmacist twice before it reaches the counter, and every compound is mixed to order in our on-site lab, not shipped in from a central facility.

  • Board-certified compounding pharmacists on staff
  • Same-day fills on standard prescriptions
  • Free local delivery within 5 miles
  • Private consultation room
