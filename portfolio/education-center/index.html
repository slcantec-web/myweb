01Lesson 01 — The Center

Same tutor, every session

Compass & Chalk opened in 2015 on one rule: a student keeps the same tutor for as long as they're enrolled, not a rotating roster of whoever's free that afternoon. Continuity is most of what actually moves a grade.

Every student starts with a 45-minute diagnostic before their first lesson, so the plan is built around what's actually confusing them — not a generic packet handed to every kid in the room.

  • One assigned tutor per student, every session
  • Diagnostic assessment before the first lesson
  • Plain-language progress reports after every visit
  • Small group and one-on-one options
