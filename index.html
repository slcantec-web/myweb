About CloudeBase

CloudeBase provides modern web solutions for small and medium-sized businesses. We help you establish your online presence with professional websites and cloud-based business applications that are affordable, secure, and easy to use.

We work under two service categories: one-time website design & development for businesses like restaurants, hotels, salons, pharmacies, tailoring shops, retail stores, educational institutes, travel agencies, and corporate companies — and subscription-based cloud business systems (SaaS) that replace the need to buy, host, and maintain your own software.

Our long-term vision is to become a cloud-first software company providing affordable digital solutions for Sri Lankan SMEs, built on reusable multi-tenant platforms so every new customer can be onboarded quickly.
